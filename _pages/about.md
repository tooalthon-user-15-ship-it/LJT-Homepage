About Me
======
I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He (who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University). I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a Bachelor of Engineering degree.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
